# Survey corpus

[survey-corpus.csv](survey-corpus.csv) is the 240-record supplementary corpus accompanying [the survey](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10462-026-11700-9).

- **Source:** [Publisher-hosted supplementary CSV](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1007%2Fs10462-026-11700-9/MediaObjects/10462_2026_11700_MOESM1_ESM.csv).
- **Literature cutoff recorded in the corpus:** 26 July 2026.
- **Audit date recorded in the corpus:** 24 August 2026.
- **Retrieved for this collection:** 4 November 2026.
- **Publication status in the snapshot:** 174 published works, 65 preprints, and one technical/web record.

The file is preserved as downloaded. The topic pages provide a reading index over its records, including cross-cutting section assignments. Their benchmark groups follow the survey's evaluation dimensions. Counts across topic pages overlap.

| Fields | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `title`, `authors`, `year` | Bibliographic identity |
| `publication_status`, `publication_type`, `venue`, `publisher_or_index` | Publication metadata at the survey cutoff |
| `doi`, `doi_url`, `arxiv_id`, `arxiv_url`, `verification_url` | Persistent identifiers and source records |
| `primary_survey_location`, `all_survey_locations` | Primary and cross-cutting section assignments |
| `inclusion_rationale` | Relevance to the survey |
| `review_cutoff`, `audit_date` | Dates associated with the published corpus |

New resources can be added to the Markdown catalogs through [contributions](../CONTRIBUTING.md); this CSV remains the survey snapshot. Existing metadata reflects the source corpus and may precede later publication updates.
